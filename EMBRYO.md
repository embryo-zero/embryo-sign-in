# Embryo Sign-In

Embryo Sign-In is Embryo-Zero's fork of [keycloak](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak) (44ec92be, commit 44ec92be). It does single sign-on for all our tools.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
